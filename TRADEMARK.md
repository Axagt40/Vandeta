# Vandeta trademark

**Vandeta\u2122 is an unregistered trademark of the Vandeta project**
(<https://github.com/Axagt40/Vandeta>). The \u2122 symbol asserts the claim; it does
not mean the mark is registered, and no trademark application has been filed
yet. This file records the policy that applies either way.

## What the licence does and does not give

`LICENSE` (AGPL-3.0) covers the **code**. It grants no right to use the
**Vandeta name or the crown artwork** to endorse or promote a modified version,
or to suggest that a build is official. Forking is welcome - under a name of
your own.

## Allowed without asking

* Naming the project: "built on Vandeta", "a fork of Vandeta", "Vandeta 1.4".
* Redistributing an **unmodified** release image with the name intact.
* Referring to the name in reviews, articles, videos and comparisons.

## Not allowed without written permission

* Publishing a **modified** build under the Vandeta name or crown logo.
* Using the name, the crown or a confusingly similar mark for a product,
  service, domain or account in a way that suggests official status.

## Registering it

Use of the name in trade already creates unregistered rights in the markets
where the firmware is offered; registration makes them cheaper to enforce. Two
routes cover this project:

| Route | Office | Scope |
|-------|--------|-------|
| EU trade mark (EUTM) | EUIPO, Alicante | one filing, all EU member states |
| National trade mark | PRH, Helsinki | Finland only |

Filing content:

* Mark: the word **VANDETA** in standard characters (a figurative version with
  the crown is a second, optional filing).
* Classes: **9** (downloadable software and firmware) and **42** (software
  design and development).
* Applicant: the project owner, individual or company, with address and e-mail.
* Specimen of use: the GitHub release page or <https://axagt40.github.io/Vandeta/>.

Both offices publish current fees on their own sites; the EUTM route is one
application for the whole EU and the PRH route is the cheaper single-country
option. Neither can be filed by the project tooling - the applicant has to sign
and pay.
